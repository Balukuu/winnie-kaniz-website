About the Space

Welcome to The Executive Retreat — a refined luxury apartment designed for discerning guests who value privacy, style, and space. This 2-bedroom residence features contemporary interiors and premium furnishings that create a serene atmosphere from the moment you arrive.

What sets this apartment apart is its private backyard — an exclusive outdoor space perfect for quiet mornings with coffee, evening sundowners, or simply unwinding in fresh air. The master bedroom comes with a private en-suite bathroom for ultimate comfort and privacy.

Equipped with a 55-inch Smart TV and Bluetooth sound system, this apartment caters to both your leisure and professional needs. Located in the heart of Bugolobi, you are surrounded by Kampala's finest dining, shopping, and entertainment options.
