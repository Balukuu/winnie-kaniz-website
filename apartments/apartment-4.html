About the Space

Welcome to The Urban Sanctuary — an intimate, beautifully furnished one-bedroom apartment that makes the most of every corner. Perfect for solo travelers and couples, it pairs a calm, restful bedroom with a bright living space and the rare luxury of two full bathrooms.

The living room is anchored by a marble-accented feature wall, warm timber detailing, and a Smart TV, opening onto a compact, fully-equipped kitchenette with a gas cooker and oven. A standout feature is the 24-hour standby power backup, so your comfort never depends on the local grid.

Set in the prestigious Bugolobi neighborhood, you are moments from Village Mall and Jazz Supermarket, and surrounded by some of Kampala's finest dining, including Shaka Zulu restaurant and a range of international eateries.
